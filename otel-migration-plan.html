          parse_from: attributes.time

Rollback at Every Phase

Phase 1 Rollback

Stop and remove the OTEL Collector containers. The old pipeline was never modified — it never stopped running.

Risk: Zero

Phase 2 Rollback

Remove the OTEL gems from Gemfile and redeploy. Logstash and Elasticsearch are still running. Log gap: none.

Risk: Zero

Phase 3 Rollback

Restart Logstash and Elasticsearch containers. OTEL Collector continues. Rollback is possible as long as the Elasticsearch volume is not deleted.

Risk: Low (keep volume)
