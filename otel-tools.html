Quick Pick Guide
Need Recommended picks
Fully open source, self-hosted, all signals SigNoz (all-in-one) — or — Grafana OSS stack (Tempo + Prometheus + Loki + Grafana)
Best trace query UX Honeycomb (BubbleUp), Tempo + TraceQL, or SigNoz
Best metrics ecosystem Prometheus + Grafana (OSS) or Grafana Cloud
Broadest integrations, enterprise scale Datadog or Dynatrace
Most affordable SaaS at high ingest volume Grafana Cloud, Coralogix, or Axiom
High-cardinality trace debugging Honeycomb
